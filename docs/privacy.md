# ASH Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

ASH is designed so that your information never leaves your phone.

## What ASH collects

Nothing. ASH does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. The app is installed without the Android `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot connect to any server.

## What ASH stores on your phone

Only what you allow, stored locally and encrypted:

- **Notifications** from the apps you turn on (for example bank or shopping apps), and the records ASH extracts from them. One-time passwords and similar codes are ignored and never stored.
- **Documents** you add to the Vault. They are encrypted with keys protected by your phone's secure hardware.
- **Reminders, vehicles, insurance policies, warranties, subscriptions and profiles** that you create or confirm.
- **Local diagnostics and logs.** These are counters and redacted log lines that stay on the phone unless you export them.

## Permissions

- **Notification access** (optional): lets ASH read notifications from apps you choose.
- **Notifications** (optional): lets ASH show reminders.
- **Camera** (optional): used only when you scan a document.
- **Biometric**: used for app lock and to open the Vault.

ASH does not request SMS, contacts, location or storage permissions.

## Backups

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for ASH. You can export a backup yourself. It is encrypted with a password you choose and saved where you choose.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Backup and data → Delete all data permanently removes everything ASH stored. Uninstalling ASH also removes all of its data.
