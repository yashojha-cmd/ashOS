# ashOS Privacy Policy

_Last updated: 10 October 2026_

ashOS is designed so that your information never leaves your phone.

## What ashOS collects

Nothing. ashOS does not collect, transmit, sell or share any data. The app is installed without the Android `INTERNET` permission, so it cannot connect to any server.

## What ashOS stores on your phone

Only what you allow, stored locally and encrypted:

- **Notifications** from all your apps by default ("Read all apps", which you can turn off or limit per app). ashOS keeps only the records it understands (bills, payments, trips, deliveries, renewals). Notifications it does not understand, such as chats, are counted and dropped: no text, no row. One-time passwords and similar codes are never stored.
- **Documents** you add to the Vault. They are encrypted with keys protected by your phone's secure hardware.
- **Reminders, vehicles, insurance policies, warranties, subscriptions and profiles** that you create or confirm.
- **Local diagnostics and logs.** These are counters and redacted log lines that stay on the phone unless you export them.

## Permissions

- **Notification access** (optional): lets ashOS read notifications from apps you choose.
- **Notifications** (optional): lets ashOS show reminders.
- **SMS** (optional): only if you tap "Import past SMS". ashOS reads bank, bill and travel SMS from business senders on this phone to fill in earlier months. Messages from personal numbers are skipped. Nothing is sent anywhere.
- **Camera** (optional): used only when you scan a document.
- **Biometric**: used for app lock and to open the Vault.

ashOS does not request contacts, location or storage permissions, and it can never send or receive SMS.

## Backups

Android cloud backup and device-to-device transfer are disabled for ashOS. You can export a backup yourself. It is encrypted with a password you choose and saved where you choose.

## Deleting your data

Settings → Backup and data → Delete all data permanently removes everything ashOS stored. Uninstalling ashOS also removes all of its data.
